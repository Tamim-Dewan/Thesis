This document presents the mathematical formulation of the Proactive Golden-Hour Bridge Model (PGBM).

\section{Survivor Priority and Service Value}

For each detected survivor $i$, let $\sigma_i \in [0,1]$ denote
severity, $c_i \in [0,1]$ denote detection confidence, and
$t_i^{det}$ denote the detection time. The time-dependent urgency
of survivor $i$ at decision time $t$ is defined as

\begin{equation}
u_i(t)
=
\sigma_i
\exp\left[-\alpha\left(t-t_i^{det}\right)\right],
\qquad
\alpha=\frac{\ln 2}{T_{half}},
\end{equation}

where $T_{half}$ is the urgency half-life. Thus, urgency decreases
exponentially as the survivor waits for assistance.

To jointly account for survivor urgency and detection reliability,
the effective service value is defined as

\begin{equation}
V_i(t)
=
\frac{2u_i(t)c_i}{u_i(t)+c_i},
\qquad
V_i(t)\in[0,1].
\end{equation}

The harmonic-mean formulation ensures that a survivor receives a
high service value only when both urgency and detection confidence
are sufficiently high. A low value of either component reduces
$V_i(t)$.

Let

\begin{equation}
x_i =
\begin{cases}
1, & \text{if survivor } i \text{ is served},\\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}

The primary assignment objective is therefore to maximize the total
effective service value of the survivors served by the available
Responder UAVs:

\begin{equation}
\max
\sum_{i\in S(t)} V_i(t)x_i.
\end{equation}

The objective is subject to UAV payload, inventory, battery, and
3D reachability constraints. Hence, energy is treated as a
feasibility constraint rather than an independent optimization
objective. Confidence thresholding may additionally be applied as

\begin{equation}
c_i \geq c_{\mathrm{thresh}},
\qquad \forall i \text{ assigned to a tour}.
\end{equation}
